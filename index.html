Theoretical Foundation

HCI Heuristics & Ethical Frameworks Reference

Theoretical frameworks utilized to design and audit the PrivacyGuard UX architecture.

Brignull's Deceptive Patterns

Taxonomy of manipulative interface mechanics including Confirmshaming (emotional guilt), Visual Interference (contrast asymmetry), and Obstruction (Roach Motel cancellation friction).

Ref: Brignull, H. (2010). Deceptive Design.
7 Privacy by Design (PbD) Principles

Embeds privacy directly into system architecture. Enforces Privacy as the Default (Principle 2) and Positive-Sum UX (Principle 4: zero functionality penalties for exercising user privacy rights).

Ref: Cavoukian, A. (2009). Information & Privacy Commissioner.
IEEE 7000 & EU AI Act

Establishes human agency, algorithmic explainability, client-side differential privacy, and unbundled consent for foundation model training datasets.

Ref: IEEE 7000-2021 & Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
