## 🏆 TOP 5 VIVA DEFENSE QUESTIONS & HIGH-SCORING ANSWERS

### Q1: What is the formal difference between bad usability and a Dark Pattern?
> **Answer:**  
> *"Bad usability is accidental friction caused by flawed heuristics, lack of user testing, or poor engineering. A **Dark Pattern (Deceptive Design)** is deliberate friction engineered with intentional cognitive asymmetry to manipulate user psychology for business profit at the direct expense of user privacy and autonomy (Harry Brignull, 2010)."*

### Q2: What does "Positive-Sum, not Zero-Sum" mean in Privacy by Design?
> **Answer:**  
> *"Ann Cavoukian’s Principle 4 states that privacy should not involve trade-offs. In a Zero-Sum design, companies claim: 'Surrender your data or you cannot use our app.' In a Positive-Sum ethical design, both privacy and utility coexist. Users who opt out of analytics still receive 100% full application functionality without penalties."*

### Q3: Why are pre-ticked checkboxes considered legally and ethically invalid?
> **Answer:**  
> *"Pre-checked checkboxes exploit human cognitive inertia and status-quo bias. Under the CJEU **Planet49 ruling** (Case C-673/17) and **GDPR Article 4(11)**, valid consent requires an active, unambiguous opt-in gesture. Pre-ticked defaults fail the legal test of freely given consent."*

### Q4: Which Jakob Nielsen Heuristic is violated by Confirmshaming?
> **Answer:**  
> *"Primarily **Heuristic #3 (User Control and Freedom)** and **Heuristic #2 (Match between System and Real World)**. Confirmshaming strips the user of neutral system controls by attaching emotional guilt and social judgment to a basic exit action."*

### Q5: Why is unbundled consent required for AI model training (Topic 5)?
> **Answer:**  
> *"Under the EU AI Act and IEEE 7000-2021, using an AI tool for task completion should not force the user to surrender private prompts, medical data, or proprietary code into foundation training datasets. Consent for model retraining must be granular, explicit, and independently revocable."*
